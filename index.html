About DRT Engraving

Where craft meets precision

DRT Engraving is a small workshop with a big love for detail. Using a modern laser engraving machine, we etch crisp, permanent designs into natural wood — bringing warmth and character to every piece we make.

Every order is handled personally, so whether it's a single keepsake or a batch for an event, you get the same care and attention to detail.

  • ◆

    Laser Precision

    Sharp, consistent detail down to the finest line and lettering.

  • ▲

    Natural Material

    Real wood grain means no two pieces are ever exactly alike.

  • ✦

    Fully Customisable

    Your names, dates, logos and artwork — engraved just for you.
